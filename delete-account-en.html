Request FamilyStars Account and Data Deletion

FamilyStars — Developer: NOOR ALDIN ALNASSAR. Document version: 1.0.0-rc.1. Prepared on 11 November 2026. Effective date: to be set after approval.

Email your request

Official request channel: familystars.support@gmail.com.

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app or first deleting family data in it. Use the account email if possible. If you have lost access, tell us so that proportionate verification can be arranged without unnecessary information.

What to include

  • The app account email and storage mode: Developer Cloud, Private Cloud or Local, mentioning LAN if used.
  • Whether you want the app account and associated data deleted, or a specified family's data deleted while keeping the account.
  • The family name or identifier if available, only as needed to identify the scope. If there are several families, identify those concerned or request review of all links.
  • Whether you already deleted family data in the app. This is optional and not a condition of requesting deletion.
  • If requesting on behalf of a child, your authority as guardian and only brief information necessary to identify the child.

Do not send passwords, app PINs, Google codes, valid invitations, Firebase keys or unsolicited identity documents.

Timing and verification

The proposed commitment is to process requests and carry out deletion within the developer's responsibility within 30 days of receipt, with proportionate verification of identity and authority and protection of others' rights. Correspondence does not restart that period. Approval of this commitment requires evidence of the operating procedure; this review copy does not confirm it is operational.

What is deleted?

In Developer Cloud, an account-deletion request covers the Firebase Authentication account and associated data and links under the developer's management, after identifying them and checking other family members' rights. An authorized family-deletion request covers the shared family space concerned, not other families.

The app's family-data deletion button does not automatically delete Firebase Authentication accounts. Account deletion does not delete Google or Gmail. You do not need to be founder to request your account's deletion, but deleting the whole shared family requires authority and checking members' rights. One person's request does not justify deleting others' data.

What may remain?

A limited Developer Cloud security record may remain to detect abuse and protect the service: family identifier, email and device-identifier hashes, dates, member counts, deletion outcome and failure stage. It contains no task text or children's names. Hashes are not fully anonymous. The approved proposed retention ceiling is 270 days from record creation; evidence of cleanup is required before publication approval. This does not justify keeping the Auth account or original family content for that period.

Service-provider traces may remain within their erasure and backup cycles. Firebase states that Authentication information may take up to 180 days to be removed from live and backup systems after user deletion is initiated. This provider period differs from our fulfilment period and the security record.

Private Cloud and local copies

The private Firebase project owner manages deletion of family data, Authentication accounts and residual records in that project. The developer cannot delete data without administrative authority and can provide guidance without requesting keys or administrative access. Deleting an entire Firebase project is not necessary to delete one account or family.

Manage local data on devices holding it through app functions or Android settings. Other LAN devices, offline devices or Android backups may hold copies. Deleting one copy, disconnecting a device or uninstalling does not prove that other copies or cloud data were erased. Review your device and backup-service settings too.
