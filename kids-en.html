FamilyStars Rules for Children

Document version: 1.0.0-rc.1. Prepared on 11 November 2026. Effective date: to be set after approval.

A small star, a lovely step

FamilyStars helps you organize your day. Your family chooses suitable tasks, like putting toys away or reading a story. When you finish and record a task under your family's rules, you collect stars and follow the agreed rewards. Stars encourage you. You matter more than any number of stars!

Tasks must be safe

Ask if you do not understand. You can say, “This is too hard for me” or “I need help.” Do not do a task that hurts you or anyone else. Do not give anyone your password or sign-in code. Do not put your home address or secrets in a task.

Who can help me now?

If a task, reward or administrator's behavior worries you, tell an adult you trust. You can ask before doing the task. You do not need to finish it to speak up. If that adult is the person worrying you, talk to another trusted adult.

A report button is planned for later

In-app reporting is not available in 1.6.74. Once the feature is available and tested:

  • You can report a task, reward or administrator without finishing the task first.
  • Reported content will disappear from your page and task wheel. Hiding a task will not remove it from the completion-percentage calculation.
  • A family administrator will review the report and may change or delete the content or reject the report.
  • You can withdraw your report and show the item again.
  • Reports stay in the family and are not automatically sent to the app developer. Family administrators may see them, so do not think they are secret from them.
  • Reporting does not automatically remove an administrator's permissions.

If you feel unsafe

Move away from danger if you can and ask a trusted adult for help immediately. Do not wait for a button, a star or an email reply.

These rules explain things

Reading these rules is not legal consent to use your data. Your parent or guardian handles necessary consent and protection. They can read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

With an adult's help, contact the developer at familystars.support@gmail.com.
