FamilyStars Terms of Use

Document version: 1.0.0-rc.1. Prepared on 11 November 2026. Effective date: to be set after approval. Reference app version: 1.6.74.

1. Purpose and developer

FamilyStars | نجوم الأسرة organizes family tasks, stars, rewards and challenges. It is for children aged 6 and over under parental supervision, adults and administrators. Developer: NOOR ALDIN ALNASSAR. Contact: familystars.support@gmail.com.

These are proposed terms for review. Preparing them does not mean the current app records acceptance or provides the planned reporting system.

2. Founder and administrator responsibilities

Founders and administrators set up the family, check the people and devices they link, assign appropriate permissions, and protect accounts and invitations. Do not create a child's data or link their device without appropriate authority, and obtain any necessary parental consent. Creating a family does not give ownership of others' data or remove their rights.

Tasks must suit the child's age, abilities, health and time. Explain tasks and rewards fairly and clearly. Stars are encouragement; never use them to withhold basic needs or punish requests for help.

3. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Insults, bullying, threats, humiliation, violence, exploitation, or encouraging harm to oneself or others.
  • Harmful sexual content, any child sexual exploitation, or requests for inappropriate images or information.
  • Dangerous or unlawful tasks, instructions to conceal harm or confront an abusive person.
  • Requests for passwords, sign-in codes, addresses, health details or other sensitive information unnecessary for the task.
  • Misusing invitations or permissions or accessing another family without authorization.

An ordinary educational task could be putting toys away in an age-appropriate way. Threatening a child or assigning dangerous work is unacceptable. Difficulty or reluctance alone does not establish abuse; every concern deserves calm review without blaming the child.

4. The right to help and to report

A child may raise concerns about a task, reward or administrator's conduct and ask a trusted adult for help. Completing the task first is not required. There must be no retaliation for a good-faith report. Version 1.6.74 does not provide the internal reporting system below. Children can seek a trusted adult's help; where needed, users can contact the developer outside the app at the official email without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Planned reporting system — unavailable in 1.6.74

Once implemented and tested, the feature will allow reports about tasks, rewards and administrators within the family space. Reported content will be hidden from the child's interface and task wheel; hiding a task will not remove it from the completion-percentage calculation. Children can withdraw the report and show the item again.

An administrator reviews the report in an appropriate time and may edit or delete content or reject the report with a suitable explanation. Reports remain within the family and are not automatically sent to the developer or another party. Administrator permissions are not automatically revoked. If the concern involves that administrator, seek another trusted adult; do not assume reports are confidential from family administrators.

This describes a future design, with no promised release date. The effects of rejecting a report and redisplaying content will be reviewed before release. These terms alone do not establish compliance with user-generated content moderation requirements.

6. Data, storage and deletion

Data is processed under the Privacy Policy. Storage and deletion control differ between Local, LAN, Developer Cloud and Private Cloud. Private project owners manage their permissions, authentication and deletion. PIN protection is not independent technical isolation for each child, and disconnecting a device does not guarantee erasure of its copy.

See Account and Data Deletion. Shared family deletion differs from Firebase Authentication account deletion; neither deletes a Google or Gmail account. These terms do not waive statutory data rights.

7. Service and liability limits

The app is an organization and motivation tool, not an emergency service, treatment, investigator of family abuse or monitor of administrators. Errors and interruptions may occur; continuous connectivity or complete restoration is not guaranteed. Administrators remain responsible for safe tasks and reviewing achievements and reports. These terms do not exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded or reduce statutory consumer or children's rights.

If there is danger, seek a trusted adult or appropriate local emergency assistance immediately; do not wait for email or a family report review.

8. Changes and acceptance

The terms' version and effective date will be updated when changes are approved. Material changes will be presented and fresh acceptance requested from those concerned before continuing the relevant activity. Acceptance is not requested at every login. Children receive age-appropriate explanations; acknowledging them does not replace lawful parental consent. Acceptance interfaces and their records are a later phase.

9. Contact

For support, enquiries, rights requests and reports sent to the developer: familystars.support@gmail.com. Email is separate from the planned family reporting system and is handled under the Privacy Policy.
